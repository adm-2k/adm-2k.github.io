This site got a full rebuild. The first version went up right after
graduation, when the plan was a straight line into interdisciplinary
research. A year later the work looks different — more building, more
teaching, more organizing — and the site should say so.

## What changed

The old site led with identity ("researcher, linguist, technology
enthusiast") and asked visitors to click around to find out what that meant.
The new one leads with evidence: the [work page](/work/) is a record of real
projects with real users — a participant-management platform in production,
open-source contributions, workshop curricula, newsroom product design —
each with dates, numbers, and links.

## What stayed

The blog. This is still a plain-Markdown Jekyll site on GitHub Pages: writing
a post means adding one file to `_posts/` and pushing. No build pipeline to
babysit, no CMS. That low floor is the point — the easier it is to post, the
more likely I am to actually do it.

## Colophon

Custom Jekyll theme, no JavaScript, two typefaces
([Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces) and
[Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)), dark mode via
`prefers-color-scheme`. Source is
[on GitHub](https://github.com/adm-2k/adm-2k.github.io), as ever.
