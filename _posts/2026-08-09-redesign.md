The first version of this site went up the month I graduated, built around
the assumption that I was headed straight into research. However, after my year in
Washington, I rebuilding the site to match my emerging interests.
The [work page](/work/) now reflects ongoing projects, and the [resume page](/resume/)
has a more concise outline of my work experience.

It's a custom Jekyll theme on GitHub Pages, set in
[Fraunces](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fraunces),
[Newsreader](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Newsreader), and
[Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter), with a few small
enhancements—cross-page view transitions, a theme toggle, some scroll
choreography—that degrade cleanly on older browsers. These posts will be
simple Markdown files meant for quickly sharing my thoughts and work. A
full website with more experimental design and interactivity is in the works,
but this is a good place to start.
