## Software

### Participant-management platform — The Upskilling Labs
<p class="meta">Next.js · 2026– · in production</p>

The Upskilling Labs holds twice-weekly technology and project-management
workshops, about thirty sessions and five hundred participants in a cycle.
I build and maintain the platform the program runs on: registration and
application intake, milestone tracking, feedback collection, and user
management across the participant lifecycle. Most weeks that also means
content updates, small fixes, and support.

### CIB Mango Tree — Civic Tech DC
<p class="meta">Python, NLP · open source · 2025–</p>

A tool for detecting coordinated inauthentic behavior in social-media
datasets, developed in public by volunteers at
[Civic Tech DC](https://civictechdc.org). So far I've contributed tokenizer
improvements, most of the current project documentation, and a whitepaper
proposing multilingual support. With the organization's
director I am also planning a Civic Tech Academy, an educational program
meant to move skills between project teams.

### Media-intake automation — Storyboard Studios
<p class="meta">Python, Bash · 2025</p>

During pre-production on a documentary I wrote scripts that automated the
studio's media intake — ingestion, tagging, and download handling. Intake
time fell by roughly forty percent, and the records for two concurrent
projects stayed consistent enough that the production team could actually
find things.

## Teaching & organizing

### GitHub for Team Collaboration
<p class="meta">Curriculum & instruction · recurring</p>

A workshop series I lead at The Upskilling Labs on version control and
collaborative development: branching, issues, pull requests, review. It
assumes no programming background and runs on real repositories rather than
slides. The materials are public:
[workshop-template](https://github.com/adm-2k/workshop-template) and
[GitHub-2.0-Workshop](https://github.com/adm-2k/GitHub-2.0-Workshop).

### Programming & events — The Upskilling Labs
<p class="meta">Working board · 2026–</p>

I co-run the workshop program end to end: scheduling and logistics for
twice-weekly sessions, mentor and partner outreach, a three-month curriculum
written with my co-lead, and the memos and documentation that keep
facilitators and participants oriented across twenty-plus events.

## Research

### Public-health discourse — Columbia Health Language Lab
<p class="meta">CDC-funded · Python, NLP · 2021–2023</p>

As a research assistant on a CDC-funded project I compiled and analyzed a
corpus of more than a hundred thousand documents, tracking shifts in public
discourse around health. I also kept the team's shared datasets and
documentation in order and presented findings, with original visualizations,
to faculty in several departments.

### The language of party platforms — Laidlaw Fellowship
<p class="meta">WZB Manifesto Project corpus · Python · 2020–2021</p>

A two-summer fellowship measuring how five German political parties
differentiate themselves in language, using the WZB Manifesto Project
corpus. I wrote the proposal, ran the analysis in Python, and presented the
results at an undergraduate research fair.

## Design

### Product design — Columbia Daily Spectator
<p class="meta">Section lead · 2020–2023</p>

Three years on the product team of Columbia's independent student newspaper,
the last as Product Design Section Lead. I ran a cross-functional group of
twelve designers and sixteen developers, built the training program for ten
incoming designers, and sat between design and engineering during the
platform's React migration. The section's tools —
[The Shaft](https://theshaft.info/) for housing,
[LionClubs](https://www.columbiaspectator.com/spectrum/2021/06/08/lionclubs-columbias-new-go-to-streamlined-club-database/)
for student organizations,
[CULPA](https://culpa.info/) for course reviews — served over three hundred
thousand readers a year.
